A notebook that feels like paper. Write, sketch, and speak your thoughts into real pages — on a shelf of notebooks that are yours alone.

Download on the App Store

Everything a real notebook does. And then some.

📕

Notebooks & covers

Build a shelf of leather-bound notebooks. Pick a cover color, add a cover photo, and choose your paper — ruled, or something all your own with a custom paper background.

📖

Pages that turn

Flip through your notebook with a 3D page-turn animation and the soft sound of paper. Not Buk remembers the page you left off on.

🎤

Voice notes, transcribed

Tap the mic and talk. Your voice note lands on the page along with a transcript — generated entirely on your device. Your audio never leaves your phone.

📷

Photos on the page

Pull photos from your library straight onto a page, like tucking a snapshot between the leaves of a diary.

✏️

Write & sketch

Type along the ruled lines or draw freely in ink — doodles, diagrams, margin notes. Finger or Apple Pencil, it all stays on the page.

⚡

Quick capture

A thought arrives, you catch it. One tap records a voice note or jots a line straight onto today's page in your diary — no digging through menus.

🔒

Lock your diary

Some notebooks are private. Lock any notebook behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

📤

Export pages

Share any page as a crisp image — paper texture, handwriting, photos, and transcripts, exactly as they appear in your notebook.

Private by design

Your notebooks live on your device, not on our servers — because we don't have any. No accounts required, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Even voice transcription happens on-device using Apple's speech frameworks.
